What it does, and only this

  • Trims spaces (including non-breaking spaces and line breaks inside a cell).
  • Lower-cases emails and lists any that are not shaped like name@domain.tld.
  • Writes phones as +country number when the number is international already, or when you chose a country. Anything else is listed, not guessed.
  • Writes dates as yyyy-mm-dd when the order is clear. A value like 03/04/2024 could be 3 April or 4 March, so it is left as found unless you choose the date order.
  • Removes empty rows and exact duplicate rows (first one kept). Rows that share an email but differ elsewhere are listed, never merged: which one wins is your decision.

What it does not do

  • It reads CSV only (export Excel files as CSV first). A number Excel has already turned into 4.05E+14 cannot be restored. The tool leaves such a cell as found and lists it in issues.csv (scientific_notation) when the exponent is 10 or more, so ask for that column exported as text; shorter forms like 2E+3 are not listed.
  • It does not import into Xero, QuickBooks, HubSpot or anything else, and it does not know your business rules.
  • It was tested against invented files only, never against anyone's real data. Check the log before you rely on a file.
